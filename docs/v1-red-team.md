# V1 Red-Team: What Is Actually Useful First

The first version should not feel like a complete operating system. It should help Ben make better relationship decisions this week.

## Keep

- One ranked shortlist of people worth thinking about next.
- Separate scores, especially relationship warmth, timing, credibility, product relevance, Foundation relevance, and evidence confidence.
- A clear recommendation: now, soon, build relationship first, hold, or do not approach yet.
- Warmest introduction path.
- Direct contact routes: email, mobile, social profile, public website, or introduction route.
- Human approval before outreach.
- Evidence and privacy review locks.

## Hide Until Later

- Large source-admin surfaces.
- Daily scan machinery before real sources are connected.
- Big dashboards full of counts.
- Complex discovery pipelines before Motion Scout and LinkedIn exports are imported.
- Any automation that appears to send messages or scrape social platforms.

## Starting Workflow

1. Open the Start screen.
2. Review the top three people.
3. Pick one person.
4. Check score, timing, warm path, and red-team warning.
5. Use the contact route only if the record is not locked.
6. Record the outcome manually.

The app should earn trust before it tries to become comprehensive.
